VocalRender

Score-native singing voice synthesis for real-world composition.

VocalRender directly transforms composer-oriented symbolic scores—lyrics, MIDI pitches, note values, and tempo—into expressive singing audio without requiring phoneme-level durations or time-aligned acoustic guidance.
